Reflection

One of the biggest challenges while completing this portfolio project was making sure all of the pages connected properly and keeping the design consistent across the entire website. One of my favorite parts of the project was creating the aesthetic of the site.

While testing the website on multiple browsers and devices, I noticed that some spacing and image sizes looked slightly different depending on the browser being used. Testing on mobile devices also helped me understand the importance of responsive design because layouts that looked good on a computer screen did not always look the same on a phone. Through testing, I learned how important it is to check websites across different platforms to make sure users have a consistent experience.

Throughout the course as a whole, I learned the foundations of web development and gained experience using HTML and CSS to build functional websites. I learned how to structure webpages, style content and improve accessibility. This course also helped me better understand how websites are designed. Overall, the course gave me valuable experience that I can continue building on in future web development and technology courses.
